How calls are scored
Direction only

A call is right if the market closes on its due date on the predicted side of the entry level, by any amount. For yields, "up" means the yield rises.

Entry and exit

Entry is the latest price when the call is logged, before the outcome is known. Exit is the due date's final close; a weekend due date uses the Friday close.

Reasoning check

After scoring, the stated reason is compared with the news and data that moved the market over the call's window.
